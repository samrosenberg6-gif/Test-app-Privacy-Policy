Privacy Policy

Test app Monetization Sample · Effective date: 23 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Test app Monetization Sample handles information when you use the app. This policy reflects the current test configuration, including Google Play Billing, Firebase Authentication, and the developer-operated backend used to verify purchases and maintain entitlement state.

1. Information we process

The current test build uses Google Play Billing for subscription purchases. When a purchase is initiated or restored, the app receives purchase-related information from Google Play, including the product identifier and purchase token.

The app also uses Firebase Authentication with anonymous sign-in. This creates a temporary Firebase user identifier so the app can authenticate requests to the developer backend without requiring the tester to create an account with an email address or password.

For purchase verification, the app sends the following information to the developer backend over HTTPS: the Firebase authentication token, the app package name, the Google Play product identifier, and the purchase token.

2. How we use this information

We use this information only to operate and test the monetization flow, including purchase attribution, verification of Google Play purchase state, acknowledgement of eligible purchases, and calculation of the corresponding entitlement state.

The backend associates purchases with the authenticated Firebase user and verifies the purchase against the Google Play Developer API before granting or updating an entitlement.

3. Information stored by the developer backend

The backend may store an anonymous customer identifier associated with the Firebase user, the entitlement identifier, product identifier(s), purchase/subscription state, entitlement expiry information when applicable, and a one-way hash of the Google Play purchase token for purchase association and reconciliation.

The raw Google Play purchase token is not intentionally stored in the entitlement records described above.

4. Google Play Billing and third-party services

Payments and payment credentials are handled by Google Play Billing. The developer does not receive the payment-card or other payment credentials used to complete a Google Play transaction.

Firebase Authentication is used for anonymous authentication, and the developer backend runs on Google Cloud/Firebase services. These services process information as necessary to provide authentication, secure backend execution, purchase verification, and entitlement management.

Google Play and the applicable Google services have their own privacy policies and terms governing their handling of information.

5. Data sharing

The developer does not sell or rent personal information collected by the app.

Information is shared with Google Play and Google Cloud/Firebase services only as necessary to authenticate the tester, process or verify purchases, operate the backend, and maintain entitlement state.

The sample app does not include developer-operated advertising or analytics. It also does not intentionally collect contacts, precise location, photos, messages, or other sensitive device data.

6. Data retention and deletion

The anonymous Firebase user identifier and backend entitlement records may remain while they are needed to operate, test, reconcile, or audit the monetization flow.

The current test app does not provide user-facing account creation or an in-app account deletion workflow. Testers who need information associated with an anonymous test account removed may contact the developer using the address below.

Information retained by Google Play or Google Cloud/Firebase is subject to the applicable retention and deletion practices of those services.

7. Security

Purchase verification requests are transmitted to the developer backend over HTTPS. The backend uses the authenticated Firebase user identity to associate purchases and checks the purchase state with the Google Play Developer API before granting entitlement.

Purchase tokens are not intentionally written to analytics events. Purchase-token hashes are used for backend association and reconciliation rather than storing the raw token in entitlement records.

8. Children's privacy

The current sample is a technical demonstration application intended for software development and testing. It is not designed as a service for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality, data practices, or third-party integrations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact
