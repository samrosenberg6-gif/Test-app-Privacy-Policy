Privacy Policy

Test app Monetization Sample · Effective date: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Test app Monetization Sample handles information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

The current sample release is configured to use a mock monetization provider. In this configuration, the app does not require an account, does not request sensitive device permissions, and does not intentionally collect or send personal or sensitive user data to the developer.

The sample app does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or Firebase client services in the distributed sample configuration.

2. Google Play Billing

The project includes an optional Google Play Billing integration for testing and demonstration. The current sample release keeps live Google Play Billing disabled and uses the mock provider by default.

If a future test or production build enables Google Play Billing, transaction and purchase information is processed through Google Play as part of the billing service. The app may receive purchase-related information, including a purchase token, to handle the billing result. Google Play's handling of this information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policy and terms.

3. Data sharing

In the current sample configuration, the developer does not sell, rent, or share personal information collected by the app because the app does not intentionally collect such information.

When live Google Play Billing is enabled, information necessary for the billing transaction is handled by Google Play. Any future backend verification service connected to the app would be separately documented and reflected in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

4. Data retention and deletion

The current sample configuration does not maintain a developer-operated user account database or intentionally retain personal information collected from users.

Because the current sample does not provide account creation, there is no developer-managed app account to delete. Information handled by Google Play is subject to Google's own retention and deletion practices.

5. Security

The project is designed so that purchase tokens are not written to analytics events and, in a production architecture, are intended to be sent only to an authorized secure backend over HTTPS for verification. The current sample configuration does not connect the app to such a backend.

6. Children's privacy

The current sample is a technical demonstration app intended for software development and testing. It is not designed as a service for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality, data practices, or third-party integrations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about this app, contact the developer at the privacy contact address listed in the app's Google Play developer information.

Before publishing: replace the contact wording above with an actual monitored privacy contact email if your Play Console listing requires a specific address.
